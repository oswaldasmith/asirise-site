As I Rise

Wake up to your own voice.

As I Rise lets you record a message to yourself — a pep talk, a reminder, a gratitude list — and plays it as your alarm. Everything stays on your iPhone; there are no accounts.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

How do I set a voice alarm?

Open Record, tap the microphone, speak, and stop to save. Then open Alarms, tap +, choose a time, pick your recording under Voice alarm, and tap Create Alarm.

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

  • In iOS Settings → As I Rise, make sure alarms/notifications are allowed (Settings inside the app has a shortcut).
  • Make sure the alarm's switch is on in the Alarms tab.
  • Check your alarm volume. Alarms ring even when the phone is on silent.

Why does the alarm only play part of my recording?

iOS limits custom alarm sounds to about 30 seconds, so the alarm plays the first 29 seconds on repeat. Tap Open on the alarm to hear the full recording inside the app.

Can I use music or other audio?

Yes. In Library, tap the import button and choose a file (m4a, mp3, wav, aac, caf, aiff) from the Files app. Protected tracks, such as Apple Music downloads, can't be imported.

How do I delete my recordings?

In Library, tap the … button next to a recording and choose Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
