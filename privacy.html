Privacy Policy

As I Rise · Effective November 7, 2026

The short version: As I Rise has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Your voice recordings and alarms stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, receive, sell or share your data.

Who we are

As I Rise is an iPhone app published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Voice recordings you make in the app, and audio files you choose to import from the Files app.
  • Alarms you create: the time, label, repeat days and which recording each one uses.
  • Alarm sounds. iOS can only play a custom alarm sound from a specific folder, so the app saves a short converted copy of the recording you chose (the first 29 seconds) there.
  • A few app settings, such as whether the welcome screen has been shown.

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. The app does not connect to any server of ours, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — only to record the messages you ask it to record. Audio is captured while you are recording and saved on your device.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit) and notifications — so iOS can ring your alarm at the time you set, even when the phone is silent or locked.
  • Files — if you choose to import audio, you pick a file in the standard iOS picker. The app can read only the files you select.

You can change any of these at any time in iOS Settings → As I Rise.

Backups

Like other apps, As I Rise's data may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) depending on your own iOS settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

Delete a recording or alarm inside the app to remove it. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it stored. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

TestFlight

If you are using a TestFlight beta, Apple may collect your email address, crash reports and feedback you submit, under Apple's privacy policy. We may see the tester information and feedback Apple provides through App Store Connect. We never receive your recordings or alarms.

Children

As I Rise is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data held about you. We hold none, but if you have a question or request, email us and we'll respond.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
